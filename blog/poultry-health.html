Best Practices for Healthy Chickens

September 2026 6 min read By Kwa-Kilui Agrovet
🐔

Keeping your poultry flock healthy is essential for productivity and profitability. Whether you're raising chickens for eggs or meat, these best practices will help you maintain a healthy flock.

1. Provide Quality Nutrition

Chickens need a balanced diet for optimal health. Ensure your birds have access to:

  • Quality feeds — starter, grower, and layer feeds appropriate for their age
  • Clean water — always available and refreshed daily
  • Grit and calcium — for digestion and strong eggshells
  • Fresh greens — as a supplement for vitamins

2. Maintain Proper Housing

A clean, well-ventilated chicken house is crucial for health:

  • Ensure proper ventilation to prevent respiratory issues
  • Provide enough space — overcrowding leads to stress and disease
  • Keep the house dry and clean to prevent disease outbreaks
  • Use proper bedding materials like wood shavings or rice husks

3. Practice Biosecurity

Preventing diseases from entering your flock is key:

  • Control visitors and limit access to your poultry house
  • Disinfect equipment and footwear between flocks
  • Quarantine new birds before introducing them to the flock
  • Keep wild birds away from your chickens

4. Follow a Vaccination Schedule

Vaccinations protect your flock from common diseases:

  • Vaccinate against Newcastle Disease and Gumboro
  • Follow the recommended vaccination schedule for your area
  • Work with a qualified veterinarian for guidance
Expert Tip: Kwa-Kilui Agrovet's veterinary team can help you with vaccination schedules and health management.

5. Monitor Health Daily

Regular observation helps catch problems early:

  • Check for signs of illness like lethargy, reduced appetite, or abnormal droppings
  • Monitor egg production and quality
  • Look for signs of parasites like mites or lice
  • Act quickly if you notice any health issues
