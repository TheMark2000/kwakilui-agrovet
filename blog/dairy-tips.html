5 Tips for Increasing Milk Production on Your Farm

September 2026 5 min read By Kwa-Kilui Agrovet
🥛

Increasing milk production is a top priority for dairy farmers in Kenya. Whether you're a smallholder or commercial farmer, these practical tips will help you boost yields and improve your herd's overall health.

1. Prioritize Quality Nutrition

Nutrition is the foundation of milk production. Ensure your cows receive a balanced diet with the right mix of:

  • Energy feeds — maize silage, hay, and grains
  • Protein sources — dairy meal, cotton seed cake, or sunflower cake
  • Minerals & vitamins — calcium, phosphorus, and vitamin supplements
  • Clean, fresh water — at least 50-80 liters per cow per day

2. Follow a Strict Milking Routine

Cows thrive on routine. Milk at the same time every day (ideally twice daily) to maintain consistent production levels.

  • Maintain hygienic milking conditions
  • Use proper milking techniques to avoid injury
  • Ensure cows are calm and comfortable during milking

3. Provide Regular Health Care

Healthy cows produce more milk. Work with qualified veterinarians to:

  • Conduct regular health check-ups
  • Prevent and treat diseases early
  • Maintain proper vaccination schedules
  • Control parasites and ticks
Expert Tip: Kwa-Kilui Agrovet offers professional veterinary services including health consultations and disease management.

4. Manage Breeding Effectively

Good breeding practices ensure consistent milk production year-round.

  • Use quality breeding stock or AI services
  • Maintain a 12-14 month calving interval
  • Monitor heat cycles and breed at the right time

5. Monitor Milk Production Records

Keep detailed records of each cow's milk production. This helps you:

  • Identify high and low performers
  • Detect health issues early
  • Make informed decisions about breeding and feeding
